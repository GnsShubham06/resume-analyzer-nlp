NLP lab experiments 1–10 (B.E. Computer Engineering, Semester VII, 2026–27) – **Group 28**:
Raghav Ahuja, Shubham Bahutale, Krishna Daryani, Harsh Manchekar. Guide: Prof. Ujwalla Bharambe.

The mini-project classifies a resume into **Engineering, HR, Finance, Healthcare or Agriculture** and extracts
skills, education, job titles and organisations after removing personal details.

## Dataset

* 100 resumes, 20 per domain: 18 supplied resumes and 82 **synthetic resumes of fictional people**
  (generated by `scripts/prepare_dataset_100.py`, marked as synthetic).
* This public copy contains only the **processed, anonymised text** (`data/resume_dataset.csv`): names, e-mails,
  phone numbers and cities are masked, the original PDF files are not included, and the raw text column is blank
  for the 18 supplied resumes. The screenshots in the report use fictional resumes only.
* Resume-level split: 60 training / 20 validation / 20 test resumes (`data/processed/splits.csv`).

## Results of Experiment 10 (test set, 20 resumes)

| model | accuracy | macro-F1 | 5 supplied originals | 15 fictional |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TF-IDF + Logistic Regression | 0.90 | 0.90 | 3 / 5 | 15 / 15 |
| Fine-tuned RoBERTa-base | 1.00 | 1.00 | 5 / 5 | 15 / 15 |

Small-data warning: one test resume changes accuracy by 5 percentage points.
